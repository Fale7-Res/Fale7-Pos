## Reproduction and cause

On the Call Center desk, an order with two lines and the caller's details was wiped by one invoke of the small ghost link "إلغاء الطلب" placed under the submit button. The cart went from 2 lines to 0. `ClearOrder` also resets the customer name, phone and address. Nothing asked first, although the Cashier already asks ("إلغاء الطلب الحالي؟").

## Change

- **Confirmation:** the link now calls `RequestClearOrder`, which opens a confirmation with "الاحتفاظ بالطلب" (`ClearOrder_Keep`) and "نعم، إلغاء الطلب" (`ClearOrder_Confirm`).
- **Empty desk:** an order with no items and no caller details is reset without a question.
- **Unchanged:** what a confirmed clear does.

## Evidence and limits

- **Tests:** `CallCenterClearOrderTests` (3).
- **Native:** the confirmation appeared, keep left 1 line, and confirm cleared it to 0.
- **Limit:** not run with Narrator; the Escape key is not wired on this overlay. It has no `ModalHeaderView`, matching the Cashier's equivalent.
