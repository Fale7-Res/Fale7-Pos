src/Falah.Client.CashierWinUI/ViewModels/UsersViewModel.Actions.cs:14-15 durable keys for CreateUserRequest(InitialPin) and ChangePinRequest(CurrentPin, NewPin); src/Falah.Client.CashierWinUI/Services/RetainedRequestStore.cs:49-56,73 writes plain JSON under %LOCALAPPDATA%; checkout requests with CustomerPhone/DeliveryAddress are kept the same way (CallCenterViewModel.Checkout.cs:181).

## 5. Expected behaviour

No PIN on disk; PII protected.

## 6. Actual code-supported behaviour

Plain text until the outcome is known.

## 7. Root cause

Generic durable store without protection.

## 8. Business impact

Anyone with access to the Windows profile reads pending PINs.

## 9. Reproduction / failure scenario

Shared Windows account at the till.

## 10. Existing safeguards

Files deleted once outcome known.

## 11. Missing safeguards

The remediation below.

## 12. Recommended remediation

Do not retain PIN fields (server-issued change ticket) and protect the store with DPAPI.

No meaningful alternative identified.
