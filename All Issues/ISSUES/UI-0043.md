## Reproduction and cause

- **Order:** Call Center order #0138 was placed with area, street, building 7, floor 2, apartment 4, a landmark and delivery notes. The `Orders` row holds all of it.
- **What the lookup showed:** "العنوان: المنطقة أ - شارع الاختبار", with no building, floor, apartment, landmark or notes. An operator answering "where is my order?" read back half an address.
- **Cause:** `CallCenterView.xaml` bound two Runs to `DeliveryArea` and `DeliveryStreet` only.

## Change

- **`LookedUpAddressLine`:** shows the stored composed `DeliveryAddress`. It falls back to `DeliveryRules.ComposeDeliveryAddress` over the stored parts, and is empty for a counter order.
- **`LookedUpDeliveryNotes`:** a new line in the lookup.
- **Both lines** collapse when empty.
- **Unchanged:** no server or data change.

## Evidence and limits

- **Tests:** `CallCenterLookupAddressTests` (3), red before the change.
- **Native:** looking up #0138 now shows "العنوان: …، عمارة 7، دور 2، شقة 4 — بجوار المخبز" and "ملاحظات التوصيل: اتصل قبل الوصول".
- **Limit:** the Cashier order lookup was not part of this defect, and was not changed.
