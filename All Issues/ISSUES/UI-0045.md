## Reproduction and cause

- **What happened:** pressing "إعادة طباعة إيصال الافتتاح" or "إعادة طباعة إيصال التسليم" on a closed shift in the Shifts history queued the job (`PrintJobs` ShiftOpen and ShiftClose with `IsReprint=1`), but nothing on screen changed.
- **Cause:** `ShiftsViewModel.ReprintAsync` sets `ReprintMessage`, which is either the sent text or the server's refusal. `ShiftsView.xaml` never bound it; only the statement dialog does.

## Change

`ShiftsView` shows `ReprintMessage` under the history header (`Shifts_ReprintMessage`, polite live region).

## Evidence and limits

- **Test:** `ShiftsReprintFeedbackTests`.
- **Native:** after a reprint the screen reads "تم إرسال الإيصال للطباعة؛ لم يتم تأكيد خروج الورقة.". Both jobs exist with `IsReprint=1`.
- **Limit:** the refusal text was not provoked natively. It is the same binding.
