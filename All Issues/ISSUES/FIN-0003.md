> The defect is present in current source as described.

## 1. Original source references

Copilot register FIN-019; INDEPENDENT_AUDIT/02

## 2. Affected components

DeliveryFactsComposer; DeliveryRules.CreateCashCustody; AssignDeliveryHandler

## 3. Current code evidence

src/Falah.Infrastructure/Delivery/DeliveryFactsComposer.cs:29-30 (`PaymentAlreadySettled: order.IsPaid`, `AmountToCollect: total - refunded`); src/Falah.Domain/Delivery/DeliveryRules.cs:11-12 (non-cash method: whole amount unless settled).

## 4. Relevant historical evidence

Copilot traced the full delivery path (DeliveryViewModel, AssignDeliveryHandler).

## 5. Expected behaviour

The driver is responsible only for uncollected legs.

## 6. Actual code-supported behaviour

A split order with a pending electronic leg has IsPaid=false, so custody = whole order total, including the cash taken at the till.

## 7. Root cause

Whole-order IsPaid flag instead of per-leg collection state.

## 8. Business impact

Driver shown a shortage at settlement; wrong cash-with-drivers figures.

## 9. Reproduction / failure scenario

300 ج order, 100 ج cash at till + 200 ج InstaPay pending, delivered → custody expects 300 ج.

## 10. Existing safeguards

Custody reassignment never duplicates (DeliveryRules.cs:23-30).

## 11. Missing safeguards

Per-leg uncollected amount.

## 12. Recommended remediation

AmountToCollect = sum of uncollected legs (pending electronic leg only, or COD amount).

## 13. Alternative approaches

Disallow delivery of split orders with pending legs.

## 14. Required regression tests

Integration: split pending delivery → custody expected equals pending leg amount.

## 15. Related issues

[FIN-0001](FIN-0001.md); [FIN-0024](FIN-0024.md)

## 16. Dependencies and prerequisites

Split-payment model.

## 17. Acceptance criteria

Custody expected equals the uncollected leg amount.

## 18. Remaining uncertainty

Whether such orders occur in practice.
