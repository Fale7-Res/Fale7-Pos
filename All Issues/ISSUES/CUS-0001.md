## 2. Affected components

GET /api/customers/lookup; GET /api/customers/{id}; POST /api/customers/upsert

## 3. Current code evidence

src/Falah.Server/Program.cs:1246-1256 (lookup and get, no permission check) and :1231 (upsert); src/Falah.Infrastructure/Customers/UpsertCustomerHandler.cs:13-15 documents the deliberate absence of a permission check; CustomerDto/CustomerAddressDto (src/Falah.Application/Customers/CustomerRequests.cs:13-18) include phone and full address.

## 4. Relevant historical evidence

No additional historical evidence beyond the sources listed.

## 5. Expected behaviour

Only order-taking roles (pos / call center) read and edit customers.

## 6. Actual code-supported behaviour

Any session, including a print_agent station account, can read any customer by id and rename customers.

## 7. Root cause

Per-route gates; reference-app parity.

## 8. Business impact

Customer PII exposure under Egypt Law 151/2020; data tampering.

## 9. Reproduction / failure scenario

Print-agent token used to enumerate customers by id.

## 10. Existing safeguards

Phone normalised; unique index on phone.

## 11. Missing safeguards

The remediation below.

## 12. Recommended remediation

Require pos/call-center permissions; keep lookup for order takers.
