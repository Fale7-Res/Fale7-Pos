## Reproduction and cause

On the POS and Call Center product catalogues, offer pills reacted only to `Tapped`. Keyboard activation and UIA Invoke did nothing (`pos-05-offer-uia-invoke-before-fix.png`), and the pills overlapped the name and price area of the tile.

## Change

- Both clients: the pills sit in the tile's picture band.
- They use `Click` (`OnOfferClicked`) and carry an accessible name.
- `ApplyOffer` ignores a second activation of the same offer within 600 ms, so a double tap adds it once.
- No pricing rule changed.
