## 1. Original source references

PRODUCTION_RESILIENCE_AUDIT/05 PRA-07

## 2. Affected components

ConfirmElectronicPaymentHandler; ReportingQueryService (business-day report)

## 3. Current code evidence

src/Falah.Infrastructure/Orders/ConfirmElectronicPaymentHandler.cs:79-81 stamps the movement with `orderEntity.BusinessDayId` and `orderEntity.ShiftId`; :41-42 requires only some open shift; src/Falah.Infrastructure/Reports/ReportingQueryService.cs:180 sums movements WHERE BusinessDayId = day. Normally prevented by the PENDING_ELECTRONIC_PAYMENT critical blocker (src/Falah.Domain/BusinessDay/BusinessDayRules.cs:89-90); reachable through [FIN-0013](FIN-0013.md)'s race (checkout stamps the day at CheckoutOrderHandler.cs:164 before commit).

## 4. Relevant historical evidence

Refunds deliberately use the open day (RefundOrderHandler.cs:112).

## 5. Expected behaviour

Closed periods never move (CLAUDE.md).

## 6. Actual code-supported behaviour

Confirmation money can land in a closed day.

## 7. Root cause

Day attribution taken from the order, not the confirmation event.

## 8. Business impact

Closed day's report diverges from its snapshot.

## 9. Reproduction / failure scenario

Pending InstaPay checkout commits just after the close evaluator → confirmed next day.

## 10. Existing safeguards

Pending blocker.

## 11. Missing safeguards

Day revalidation; policy on attribution.

## 12. Recommended remediation

Fix [FIN-0013](FIN-0013.md); owner/accountant decides whether confirmations post to the confirmation day.

## 13. Alternative approaches

No meaningful alternative identified.

## 14. Required regression tests

Inject a pending order stamped with a closed day; confirm; closed day report equals snapshot.

## 15. Related issues

[FIN-0013](FIN-0013.md)

## 16. Dependencies and prerequisites

Accountant decision on attribution.

## 17. Acceptance criteria

Closed day figures unchanged after any confirmation.

## 18. Remaining uncertainty

Requires the race to occur.
