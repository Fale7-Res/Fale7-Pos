## Reproduction and cause

WinUI exposes no name for a Button whose content is a panel or icon, or for a TextBox without a Header or PlaceholderText. GridView items are named by `ToString()`, so product, category and offer tiles were read out as type names. A XAML scan listed the unnamed controls (`controls/a11y-unnamed.json`).

## Change

- `ToString`/`AccessibleName` on `ProductTile`, `CategoryTile` and `OfferChoice` in both clients. A product tile reads as "name، price".
- Variant buttons are bound to the variant name.
- 47 `AutomationProperties.Name` additions in both clients: refresh buttons, shift header buttons, cash and amount inputs, combo boxes, row buttons and checklist items.
- Lock button tooltip explains that it cannot lock during an unresolved money operation.

## Evidence and limits

- Tests: `TileAccessibleNameTests`.
- Native UIA reads during the shift and driver runs found the controls by name.
- Limit: a full screen-reader pass (Narrator) and a keyboard-only walk through every screen are not done. See `docs/UI_CONTROL_VERIFICATION_MATRIX.csv`.

## Follow-up — 2026-11-08 (later pass)

A native UIA read of the Delivery screen found its refresh button still unnamed. Its text sits inside a StackPanel, which UIA does not use as a name, and the first pass missed it. A re-scan found the same pattern in `ReportsView`. Both buttons now carry `AutomationProperties.Name="تحديث"`. The Delivery one was verified natively: it reads "تحديث" and invokes.

The re-scan reports no other nameable control. Its remaining hits are property elements, not controls.
