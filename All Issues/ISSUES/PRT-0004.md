## Remediation 2026-11-18 (audit)

`WindowsSpoolerTicketTransport.SendAsync` now watches the submitted job (`SpoolerJobWatch`): printed when it leaves the queue or is marked printed/complete; failed when the printer reports offline / work-offline / paper out / jam / door open / error / paused, when the job reports error, offline, paper out, blocked or user intervention, or when it is still queued after 15 s. Owner decision (2026-11-18): the job is left in the Windows queue; the Arabic failure message says it may still print by itself, so a reprint is checked first.

Evidence: `SpoolerJobWatchTests` (9). Still to do on site (`docs/DAILY_OPERATIONS_VERIFICATION.md`): unplug the XP-80C, open its cover and run it out of paper, and confirm what its driver reports - a driver that reports nothing shows up only as the 15 s timeout. Cover-open/paper-out reporting depends on the driver's bidirectional support.
