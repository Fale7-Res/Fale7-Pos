## Remediation record — 2026-11-08 (Claude, UI-REQ-002)

Version tested: b3071be + working tree. Full integration suite run on source fingerprint 743AFB70B1B79259.

- **Frozen at close:** the close now freezes the complete closing statement in the same transaction. That covers sales, payments, each expense, cash reconciliation, handover, open items and the business day. It is stored as JSON on the close's audit event (`ShiftCloseStatements`), with no migration.
- **Printing:** `PrintJobQueryService.ComposeShiftCloseSlipAsync` prints that frozen copy. Only a shift closed before the change is rebuilt from live records, and its paper prints a banner saying so.
- **Regression test:** `ShiftCloseStatementTests.The_close_freezes_a_statement_that_reconciles_and_never_changes_or_duplicates`. A pending InstaPay order is confirmed after the close (a late movement) and the slip is reprinted. The reprinted lines equal the original slip, plus the reprint banner (`Assert.Equal(original, reprinted…)`).
- **Pre-change shift path:** `ShiftCloseStatementTests` (second test).
- **Native evidence:** `artifacts/claude-20261107/ui/controls/shift-12…15`.
- **Physical paper:** remains an on-site check.
