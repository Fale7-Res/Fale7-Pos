## Remediation 2026-11-18 (audit)

`TreasuryBalanceQuery.LockAndReadBalanceAsync` replaces `LoadAccountMovementsForUpdateAsync` in all 16 spenders. Spenders of one account queue on a transaction-owned application lock (`FalahAccount:<id>`, 5 s); the balance is a `SUM` read under READ_COMMITTED_SNAPSHOT, so no row or range locks are taken and checkouts keep inserting their movements. Lock timeout = `BalanceLockTimeoutException` (a DomainException with the existing retry message, recognised by `BalanceLockContention`).

Evidence: `TreasuryBalanceLockTests.A_balance_check_in_progress_on_the_drawer_does_not_hold_up_a_cash_sale` - red with the old query (the sale was still waiting after 6 s), green now; `Two_spenders_of_one_account_take_turns_and_the_second_sees_the_first` green; all treasury/expense/purchasing/payroll/refund/void concurrency classes in the full run.

Still open: the SUM still scans the account's history. The account, shift and status columns are `nvarchar(max)` and cannot be index keys without narrowing them (not an additive migration) - owner question recorded in `REMEDIATION/state.json`.
