> The defect is present in current source as described.

## 1. Original source references

Copilot register FIN-003; INDEPENDENT_AUDIT/02

## 2. Affected components

Order.ApplyRefund; RefundOrderHandler

## 3. Current code evidence

src/Falah.Domain/Orders/Order.cs:240-252 has no IsPaid/collected check; src/Falah.Infrastructure/Orders/RefundOrderHandler.cs:91-93 only checks that the source account has enough posted balance.

## 4. Relevant historical evidence

No additional historical evidence beyond the sources listed.

## 5. Expected behaviour

A refund cannot exceed the amount actually collected.

## 6. Actual code-supported behaviour

An undelivered COD order (cash still with nobody) or a pending InstaPay order can be refunded from the drawer/InstaPay account if that account has balance from other sales.

## 7. Root cause

Refund rules ignore collection state.

## 8. Business impact

Direct drawer cash loss; theft opportunity.

## 9. Reproduction / failure scenario

COD delivery created (not delivered); cashier refunds it → cash leaves the drawer.

## 10. Existing safeguards

Balance guard prevents a negative account.

## 11. Missing safeguards

Collection check.

## 12. Recommended remediation

Refuse refunds unless the order is collected (IsPaid, or only confirmed legs); COD refund policy is an owner decision.

## 13. Alternative approaches

Refund limited to confirmed leg amounts.

## 14. Required regression tests

Integration: refund of undelivered COD and pending InstaPay → 422; drawer unchanged.

## 16. Dependencies and prerequisites

Owner COD refund policy.

## 17. Acceptance criteria

Uncollected refunds refused.
