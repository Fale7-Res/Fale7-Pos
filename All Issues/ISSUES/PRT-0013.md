## Fix 2026-11-22 (owner decisions)

Owner decision 2026-11-22: retry automatically; every paper printed again says "إعادة طباعة". ReportPrintJobResultHandler puts a failed job back to Pending for PrintJobs:AutomaticRetries (default 2) attempts, each after PrintJobs:RetryDelaySeconds (default 30); PrintJobQueryService marks any job with AttemptCount > 0 or IsReprint as a reprint. It is the same order however many papers come out. Tests: PrintJobAutomaticRetryTests (new); retry/claim/printing/dashboard group 100/100. On-site printer test pending.
