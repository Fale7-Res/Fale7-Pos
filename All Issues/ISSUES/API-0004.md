## Remediation 2026-11-18 (audit)

The application locks on the sales path (financial admission, stock posting, account spending) wait at most `LockTimeouts.Milliseconds` = 5 s instead of 15 s, so the server answers (done, or an Arabic "busy, send it again" that changed nothing) before the till's 10 s timeout. The full-history reads behind it are reduced by PERF-0001 and by the new date indexes (`AddDiscountLimitAndReportingIndexes`). Still open: other full-history reads, and the business-day/driver/user-identity locks still wait 15 s (off the sales path).
