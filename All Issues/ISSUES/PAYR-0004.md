> The defect is present in current source as described.

## 1. Original source references

Copilot register (cross-flow risk, policy question)

## 2. Affected components

PayEmployeeHandler; CreatePayrollHandler; PayPayrollHandler

## 3. Current code evidence

src/Falah.Infrastructure/Employees/CreatePayrollHandler.cs:48-63 freezes period lines ignoring Settlement debits and passes Money.Zero advances; src/Falah.Infrastructure/Employees/PayPayrollHandler.cs:142-151 pays frozen NetPayable without re-checking current balances.

## 4. Relevant historical evidence

Advances ARE written to the ledger (RecordEmployeeCashOutHandler.cs:122-130); payroll intentionally never deducts them.

## 5. Expected behaviour

Owner policy: either payroll nets standalone settlements/advances or they are forbidden during an open period.

## 6. Actual code-supported behaviour

Both paths pay.

## 7. Root cause

Two payout paths with no shared balance check.

## 8. Business impact

Double payment risk.

## 9. Reproduction / failure scenario

Employee paid via PayEmployee mid-month, then payroll pays full period.

## 10. Existing safeguards

None identified beyond those noted in the evidence.

## 11. Missing safeguards

The remediation below.

## 12. Recommended remediation

Owner decision; then net settlements/advances in CreatePayroll or block them.

## Fix 2026-11-22

Owner decision 2026-11-22, implemented: the run subtracts Settlement and Withdrawal debits of the period (PayrollRules.CalculatePayrollLines, PayrollLine.PaidOutsidePayroll) and at pay time caps each line at the employee's ledger balance excluding advances (PayPayrollHandler). New column PayrollLines.PaidOutsidePayrollPiastres (migration 20261122142055_AddPayrollLinePaidOutsidePayroll, additive nullable; snapshot diff 3 lines). Tests: PayrollRulesTests (2 new, RED then GREEN), PayrollVerticalSliceTests.A_run_created_after_a_payout_pays_only_what_is_still_owed and A_payout_made_after_the_run_was_created_is_not_paid_again (the second proven RED with the cap disabled: paid 20000 instead of 5000).
