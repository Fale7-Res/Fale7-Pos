## 3. Current code evidence

src/Falah.Infrastructure/Printing/PrintJobQueryService.cs:42-56 (no TOP; whole route); src/Falah.Server/Program.cs:2373 (lease 120 s); src/Falah.PrintAgent/PrintJobProcessor.cs:68-78 (claims once, prints sequentially); transport timeout 15 s (src/Falah.PrintAgent/AgentOptions.cs:52).

## 5. Expected behaviour

Each ticket printed once even with two agents per route (decision P3 allows this).

## 6. Actual code-supported behaviour

Jobs not yet printed when the lease expires are claimed by another agent.

## 7. Root cause

Unbounded batch, fixed lease, no renewal/fencing.

## 8. Business impact

Duplicate kitchen tickets after an outage.

## 9. Reproduction / failure scenario

30 queued jobs after printer recovery, 5 s each.

## 10. Existing safeguards

Mutex per machine; exclusive claim while leased.

## 11. Missing safeguards

The remediation below.

## 12. Recommended remediation

Bounded batch (TOP n) or per-job claim/renewal before sending.

## 13. Alternative approaches

No meaningful alternative identified.

## 14. Required regression tests

Two processors, 2 s lease, slow fake transport → each job once.
