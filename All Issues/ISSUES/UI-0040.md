## Reproduction and cause

`/api/orders/lookup` matched only the exact stored order number. A cashier typing what the receipt shows ("#0128", "0128", "128" or "١٢٨") got "not found" and could not refund or reprint.

## Change

- New `OrderNumberInput.TryParseSequence`. It accepts "#", spaces, leading zeros, and Arabic-Indic and Persian digits, for values greater than 0.
- The lookup falls back to the order's `SequenceNumber`.
- The 404 now says in Arabic that no order has that number and asks the cashier to check the printed receipt.
- Authorization is unchanged.
