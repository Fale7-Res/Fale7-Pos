## Reproduction and cause

- **Reproduction:** in the POS cart (and the identical Call Center flyout), tap the "حار" chip twice. The line note became "حار، حار", and that is what the kitchen ticket prints. A double tap on a touch till is ordinary.
- **Cause:** `AppendNoteText` appended without looking at what was already there.

## Change

- **`AppendNoteText`, both clients:** a chip adds its note only if the line does not already carry exactly that item.
- **Typed notes are untouched.** A typed note that merely contains the word ("حار جدا") is not treated as the chip.

## Evidence and limits

- **Tests:** `NoteChipTests` (8). The double tap failed before the fix; it passes now.
- **Native, Cashier:** a double tap gives "حار"; then "بدون بصل" gives "حار، بدون بصل".
- **Native, Call Center:** a double tap on "حار" then "بدون بصل" appends each once.
- **Trailing line break:** a typed note that ends with Enter no longer leaves the chip on a line of its own; the note is trimmed before appending. Covered by a test case.
