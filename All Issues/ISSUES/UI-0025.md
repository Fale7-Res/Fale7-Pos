## Fix 2026-11-22 (owner decisions)

Owner decision 2026-11-22: register and prepare first, the customer pays at collection; it is a sale when paid; an unpaid one blocks the day close until a manager cancels it. Checkout PayOnCollection (pickup only; CheckoutRules.BuildPlan takes no money) sets Orders.AwaitingCollection; the kitchen ticket prints, the customer receipt waits for the payment. POST /api/orders/collect-pickup (CollectPickupPaymentHandler; any method via CheckoutRules.BuildCollectionPlan; moves the sale instant and shift to the payment and keeps RegisteredAtUtc / RegisteredShiftId; posts the money as checkout does; replays by request id; row lock against two tills). Every sales read excludes AwaitingCollection orders. Close blocker UNCOLLECTED_PICKUP. POST /api/orders/cancel-uncollected-pickup (reason, stock disposition; no money moves). GET /api/orders/awaiting-collection. Migration AddPickupPayOnCollection (additive). Till: pay-on-collection switch in pickup details; collect and cancel in Order Lookup with a waiting list; the collection is kept on disk until its outcome is known. Tests: CheckoutRulesTests (+6 domain), PickupPayOnCollectionTests (5; RED: the sale counted before payment and the day closed over an unpaid pickup with the code off), PickupCollectionClientTests (5). Screen built, not run.

Owner, 2026-11-23: the cancel always takes a manager - a manager logged in at the till, or a manager's PIN (approval action "pickup_cancel") for anyone else, whatever permissions they hold (AuthorizationPolicy.ValidateApproval, no permission bypass). Tests: PickupPayOnCollectionTests A_cashier_cannot_cancel_an_unpaid_pickup_without_a_managers_pin_whatever_they_hold (RED: a cashier holding orders_refund cancelled with no PIN), 6/6; PickupCollectionClientTests (+1), client 458/458.
