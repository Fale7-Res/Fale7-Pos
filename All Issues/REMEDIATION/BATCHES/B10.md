# B10 — Print ownership and first-print duplicates

Implemented 2026-11-07; verification in progress.

- First-print creation serializes on the order row and reuses existing non-reprint jobs for each requested route. Explicit reprints remain independent. No historical production rows are changed.
- Claims select TOP1, with an opaque token rotated on acquisition. Agent result reports carry that token; stale reports append history without overwriting the current job/result.
- Before bytes leave, the agent persists its uncertain attempt and atomically marks the claimed job Sending. A Sending job is never automatically reclaimed. This prevents a slow printer or expired lease from producing a second ticket. Unfinished sends are visible in the printing overview for paper inspection.
- New durable reports carry their claim token and are replayed independently of the pending queue after restart. Legacy journal entries retain their earlier recovery path.
- Call Center customer reprint explicitly sets IncludeKitchenRoutes=false. Client RED then GREEN1/1.
