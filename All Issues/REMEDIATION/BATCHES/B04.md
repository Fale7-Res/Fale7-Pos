# B04 — Financial admission inside transactions

Implemented 2026-11-07. FinancialAdmission takes a transaction-owned SQL application lock: shared for money commands, exclusive for shift/day close. Close waits for in-flight admitted commands. Commands replay first, then re-read active day/shift, source balance or invoice debt inside the transaction. Shift float and paid purchase checks no longer run before their transaction. Supplier payment locks/reloads the invoice and rechecks remaining debt. Day close loads/transitions its day only under the exclusive barrier.
