# B01 — DST-safe business clock

**Issues:** FIN-0028 · **Started:** 2026-11-06

## Analysis (falah-analyst)

**Verdict: READY.** No migration, no contract change, no policy decision.

**FIN-0028 is still present.**
- `src/Falah.Domain/Common/BusinessClock.cs:38` (start) and `:39` (end, the next day's midnight) call `ConvertTimeToUtc` on a local midnight.
- On Egypt's DST start (the last Friday of April, 00:00 → 01:00), that midnight is invalid, so the call throws `ArgumentException`. This happens on the DST day itself (at :38) **and on the day before** (at :39).
- `UtcRangeForBusinessDates` (:47-53) and `BusinessDateRange.ToUtcRange()` (:76-77) inherit the failure.
- DST end (the last Thursday of October, 24:00 → 23:00) does not throw. That Thursday is a 25 h day.
- Zone resolution: "Africa/Cairo" falls back to "Egypt Standard Time" (:13-21). This machine's registry has the 2023 Egypt rule.

**The impact is wider than the issue states.**
- `MenuQueryService.cs:89-90` (best-sellers over 14 days) makes GET `/api/menu` return 500 on 2027-04-29, 04-30, 05-12 and 05-13. The till and Call Center cannot load the menu on those days.
- `OwnerDashboardQueryService.cs:81-87` (the comparable period, 7 days back) fails on 2027-05-06 and 05-07.
- Historical reports for 2025-04-24/25 and 2026-04-23/24 throw now.
- **The deadline is before 2027-04-29.**

**Call sites:** all of them go through `BusinessClock` (Program.cs:1934; ReportingQueryService 42, 148, 247-248, 560, 601; RefundEventQueries 24; OwnerDashboardQueryService 48, 116, 136, 272; DailyStatementQueryService 63; InventoryQueryService 57; EmployeeQueryService 29-30; EmployeeLedgerPeriods 20-21; MenuQueryService 89; client DeliveryViewModel.Statistics 87-88). One domain fix covers every one.

**Guards** (pass today):

**RED check:** (a)-(c) must fail with `ArgumentException` at `BusinessClock.cs:38/39`. An assertion mismatch instead means stale time-zone data: stop and report.

**Fix:** if local midnight is invalid, use the first valid instant. That is `new DateTimeOffset(midnight, offset just before the jump).ToUniversalTime()`. The normal path is unchanged. The comment explains why DST end needs no handling.
