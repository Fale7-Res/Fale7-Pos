# B18 — Payroll calculations, dates and terminal-state race

Implemented 2026-11-07. Adjustment credits net against adjustment/deduction debits, so attendance correction reaches net pay. Payroll uses the existing EmployeeLedgerPeriods.ForDays basis, matching employee reports: Cairo recording dates, salary accruals by their earning date. Impossible calendar months are refused before saving.

Pay and transition serialize on the same PayrollRuns UPDLOCK row. Pay loads status, approval and lines only inside its financial-admission transaction. Employee locks precede treasury balance locks, matching standalone payouts. A deterministic paused-before-transaction test proves a run cancelled in the meantime cannot be revived/paid.
